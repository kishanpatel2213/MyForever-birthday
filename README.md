## 📸 How to Add Simran's Photos

You have **two simple ways** to add her photos:

### Method 1: Instant Browser Preview (Quick & Easy)
1. Double click and open `index.html` in your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, etc.).
2. Scroll to **"The Simran Gallery"**.
3. Hover over or tap any frame and click **"📷 Select Photo"**.
4. Choose any photo from your computer or phone — it will appear immediately in that frame!

### Method 2: Permanent Files in the Folder
1. Choose 6 of her best pictures.
2. Rename them to:
   - `photo1.jpg` (or `.png`)
   - `photo2.jpg`
   - `photo3.jpg`
   - `photo4.jpg`
   - `photo5.jpg`
   - `photo6.jpg`
3. Put them into the `images/` folder inside this project folder:
   `c:\Users\Dell\Desktop\girlfriend birthday\images\`
4. Open `index.html` and update the `src` attribute (e.g. `images/photo1.jpg`), or simply save them as JPEG/PNG!

## 🎵 How to Add Your Favourite Music

1. Take your favourite MP3 song file.
2. Rename it to: **`music.mp3`**
3. Paste it directly into the `audio/` folder:
   `c:\Users\Dell\Desktop\girlfriend birthday\audio\music.mp3`
4. That's it! When Simran clicks the Play button or taps the gift box, your song will start playing!

*(You can also click the **"🎵 Change Song"** button on the floating player at the bottom right to pick any song directly from your device at any time).*
